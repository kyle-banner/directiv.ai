"Just put a human in the loop." It's the mic drop of every agentic application risk conversation.



But HITL is becoming an escape hatch to dump system design failures onto unsuspecting coworkers. Three ways HITL goes wrong: 





Responsibility Without Authority





Alarm Fatigue



Rubber-Stamp Automation



HITL works when it's deliberate: rare decisions, true exceptions, moments the system can't anticipate. If your AI system requires constant human oversight to stay safe, it will never be safe.



Human vigilance is bounded. Humans must intervene on purpose.
